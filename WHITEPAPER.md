## 1. Executive summary

MasterChef Coin (MACH) is an early-stage digital-token project inspired by professional culinary practice, culinary education, chefs, and global food culture. The project's long-term vision is to explore a digital ecosystem that can connect culinary communities with educational initiatives and other food- and hospitality-related experiences.

The project is being documented and developed incrementally. Its current existence as a token does not mean that every proposed utility or ecosystem component is already available.

## 2. Vision and mission

**Vision:** Explore ways to connect culinary knowledge, professional chefs, learners, and food culture with accessible digital tools.

**Mission:** Build a transparent, community-oriented project and evaluate practical uses of blockchain technology in support of culinary learning and participation.

The project aims to approach this mission responsibly, with clear documentation and realistic statements about what is live, planned, or still under consideration.

## 3. The opportunity

Culinary knowledge is shared through schools, professional kitchens, instructors, chefs, hospitality businesses, and online communities. Digital platforms may create additional ways for these groups to discover educational content, participate in communities, and support initiatives.

MACH is exploring whether a blockchain-based token can play a useful role in parts of this environment. A token alone does not create educational value; useful services, reliable partners, appropriate governance, and sustainable implementation would be required.

## 4. Project concept and potential utility

The following areas are under consideration. They are **proposed directions only** and should not be interpreted as active features or guaranteed benefits.

- **Culinary education:** Potential participation in cooking lessons, workshops, learning resources, or educational initiatives, subject to design and implementation.
- **Chef community:** Potential community activities that connect culinary professionals, instructors, students, and food enthusiasts.
- **Digital experiences:** Potential digital content, campaigns, recognition, or other chef-focused experiences.
- **Food and hospitality ecosystem:** Exploration of suitable collaborations with restaurants, culinary schools, instructors, and hospitality-sector participants.

Any future token utility must be described in specific terms before it is launched, including eligibility, operating rules, fees (if any), and technical requirements. No utility should be assumed to exist merely because it appears in this document.

## 6. Token economics and supply

The project currently states a total supply of **1,000,000,000 MACH** with **18 decimals**. The project owner's current allocation direction assigns **20% (200,000,000 MACH)** to the founder and team: 10% (100,000,000 MACH) for the founder and 10% (100,000,000 MACH) for the team, divided equally among three members (approximately 33,333,333.333 MACH each).

The intended founder/team lock framework is a two-year plan with an initial one-year lock and release during year two. Exact release dates, frequency, beneficiary wallet addresses, fractional-unit rounding, and implementation mechanism remain unspecified. The remaining 80% (800,000,000 MACH) has not been allocated by this founder/team plan and requires a separately approved distribution plan.

These figures describe a **project allocation plan only**. They do not establish that tokens have been segregated, transferred, vested, or locked on-chain. No on-chain lock or release is claimed. See [TOKENOMICS.md](TOKENOMICS.md) for the current detail and outstanding items.

## 8. Development approach and roadmap

The project is early-stage. The following sequence represents proposed priorities, not guaranteed delivery dates:

1. **Documentation and transparency:** Maintain the README, whitepaper, tokenomics information, official links, and risk disclosures.
2. **Community foundations:** Maintain official communication channels and publish clear community information.
3. **Utility specification:** Define a small number of practical use cases, including how they would work and what would be required to deliver them.
4. **Technical planning:** Assess contract documentation, integrations, operational requirements, and security needs.
5. **Pilot development:** Consider limited educational or community pilots only when the necessary resources and partners are available.
6. **Security and review:** Arrange independent technical review before relying on new smart-contract functionality.

No launch date, exchange listing, partnership, price target, or return is promised.
